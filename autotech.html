AutoTech

The technology arm of The Personal System.

What is AutoTech?

AutoTech is a self-defined domain of the Personal System that focuses on technology, particularly within and related to the field of computer science.

History

It emerged in September 2025 as a reaction to the increasing demand for computer science and the notion that Autodidact couldn’t sustain as the sole learning domain of the system.

Purpose

It functions as a self-learning and self-agency protocol of technology, as implicit work is expected within the field. It is the spiritual successor of Autodidact as the focused domain of Computer Science increases demand.

During 2025-2026, it replaced Autodidact to become the frontier of learning. By 2026-2027, the goal is to maximize the externalization of learning and application, as it aims to function as a platform for technology development (Frontend, Database, Backend, AI).
