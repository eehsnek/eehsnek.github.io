AutoX

The externalization arm, personal brand, and proof of work of The Personal System.

What is AutoX?

AutoX is a branch of The Personal System focused on social media, digital identity, and externalized use of digital platforms.

Purpose

AutoX exists to make social media an intentional part of the personal system rather than simply a collection of platforms used passively.

Principles

  • Build a coherent digital footprint.
  • Use platforms intentionally.
  • Understand online culture.
  • Externalize and organize digital identity.

Systems

  • Social media strategy
  • Digital identity
  • Content systems
  • Online cultural analysis
