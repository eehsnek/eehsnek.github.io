What is it all about?

The Personal System is a self-directed life operating system designed to produce consistent, compounding performance across defined domains over the long term.

It is a mental infrastructure that encapsulates principles, constraints, and defined domains, governing decisions, energy allocation, and progress measurement.

The individual of the PS is the user, the product, and the man of the infrastructure.
